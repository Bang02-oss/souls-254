# Souls 254

A public freelance marketplace connecting Kenyan freelancers with clients abroad. Clients post real jobs across 12 categories (writing, design, development, video, marketing and more); freelancers apply, get hired, and chat privately with the client inside each job. Payments happen outside the app (M-Pesa, PayPal, bank) and are recorded when the client closes the job and leaves a review. Freelancers have public profiles with ratings and an earnings page. The workspace owner (Bang.soul02@gmail.com) has an admin page to remove jobs and block users.
